2 DISCLAIMER

Before reading any further, be aware that this book is set in a different age which had different values and morals. In attempting to recapture the sense of that age, I have presented in detail situations and scenes as I believe they may have happened.

In those days, as I have tried to show, sex, violence and human sacrifice frequently constituted acts of worship of the Mother Goddess. The sex, violence and sacrifice presented in this book are not gratuitous but are essential to the story.

If you think you might find this disturbing, please put this book down now.
